# Routing policy

Classify scope, contract risk, and operational risk before selecting capabilities. Select Luna only when every axis is low. Use Terra for the normal implementation path. Use Sol to plan and independently review when contract or operational risk is elevated.

Rank skills and agents by direct relevance to the objective, project stack, and changed surface. Prefer a project-specific capability over a generic one. Prefer a trusted or bundled capability over an unknown third-party capability when relevance is otherwise equal. Do not choose more than three skills or one implementation specialist unless the task has independently separable workstreams.

The route is a decision record, not a request for approval. Continue automatically for routine workspace work. Stop before any consequential action outside the stated scope.
